RR Rusiru Rathmina — Software Engineer

Dialog.lk is Sri Lanka's largest telecom platform — 15+ million subscribers, always-on infrastructure, enterprise portals, the works. I'm a software engineer at Omobio, the company that builds and maintains it. I joined as a trainee in November 2023, became an associate engineer in 2024, and have been a full engineer since January 2026.

The job is production engineering in the real sense. I do L3 incident response — the kind that surfaces at 2 AM when a service stops responding and you're the person with context. I've migrated the platform's container images from end-of-life Debian to Alpine Linux, bringing image sizes down by 60% and deployment time down by 75%. I built PDPA consent management while Sri Lanka's data protection law was still being finalized. I've sat in ISO 27001 security audits representing the engineering team.

Good engineering is mostly care — care about what breaks, who it affects, and whether the fix holds at 3 AM six months from now.

The stack at Dialog is a mix of old and new: Drupal and Yii for the legacy platform core, React on the frontend, Kafka for event streaming, Redis for caching, AWS (S3, SES, CloudWatch) for infrastructure, Docker and Kubernetes for orchestration. I've written code in PHP, JavaScript, and configuration in everything. SonarQube is running on every commit.
